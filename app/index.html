    Road to Platinum — about 200 hours

    One playthrough, done in the right order. The platinum is simply all 54 other trophies, so the plan below is the plan for everything.

    1. Play the story, save at every chapter

      Chapters 1–4 and the final mission unlock 12 story trophies on their own. Before you start each chapter, make a manual save in a fresh slot — there is no mission replay, and that save is your only safety net if you miss something.

    2. Chapter 1 — do the balcony meditation now

      Meditate with Lucia on the Chapter 1 balcony before the chapter ends. It is one of the 18 meditation spots for Positive Mental Attitude, and it disappears after Chapter 1. This is the one trophy you can truly lock yourself out of.

    3. Chapter 2 — open the money systems

      Finish Daily Driver to unlock garage purchases in the Vice City Real Estate app (8 garages, ~$160,000 for Little Boxes). Finish Moses to open the Scooter Bros app and start registering cars for On the Spot. Start the Kingpin Bowling illegal operation with Sticky Bombs for Let's Go Bowling.

    4. Chapter 3 — jewel jobs and the timed race

      Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs gives you the Snap Gun for a clean, undetected store robbery (Sneakthief). In Yago's stranger strand, one race is time-sensitive and marked with an hourglass — save first, then race.

    5. Chapter 4 — bank vaults

      The optional mission That's Where The Money Is opens bank vaults. Rob 5 and escape for Maximum Withdrawal, and all 8 for full completion. Vault jobs hand you four stars immediately, which also sets up Hot Together — lose the heat with your partner beside you.

    6. After the story — stranger strands

      Finish 8 stranger strands for Strangers & Freaks (Andy's, Billy's and Tyrone's strands do not count — they have their own trophies). The Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy and Tyrone finales stay open after the story, so there is no deadline pressure here.

    7. The long grind — collectibles & counts

      250 stunts, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles discovered, 39 illegal operations, 3 card sets, 14 drone-race locations, 50 challenges, 20 race wins, gold medals in all 42 shooting-range challenges. These are the bulk of the ~200 hours — chip away at them between story beats instead of leaving them all for the end.

    8. The 100% push

      Open Progress → Stats → 100% Completion after the story and clear what remains: 300 stunts, 200 of 277 vehicles, all 8 vaults, all 39 operations, 70 of 80 challenges. A Long Road pops at 100%.

    Chapter-save advice

    Keep one manual save per chapter start, in separate slots, and never overwrite them until the platinum pops. If a missable slips past you, that save is the difference between replaying one chapter and replaying the whole game.

    Missables — what can actually go wrong

    Almost everything in GTA 6 can be cleaned up after the story. Only one trophy is truly missable, plus one time-sensitive step worth knowing about.

    Time-sensitive, not missable

    • Strangers & Freaks — one race in Yago's stranger strand is timed (hourglass icon). Save before it; failing it does not end the strand, but the window is real.
    • Stranger finales (Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy, Tyrone) — all stay open after the story ends, so they cannot be missed.
